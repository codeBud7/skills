# attack-the-premise

After two failed fixes, census the shared assumption and question it.

**Why:** Repeating the same class of patch burns the same hole. The imbalance usually lives in an actor you have not named.

**Apply when:** A "fix" bounced, a flake returned, or two theories both "almost" worked.

**Do:** List who holds the bad state (process, cache, clock, user, test harness). Change the premise, then the code.

**Don't:** Apply a third patch of the same shape.

**Report:** The premise you discarded and the actor you now blame.
