# exhaust-options

When there is no precedent, try two or three competing designs before committing.

**Why:** The first shape you like is rarely the smallest. Cheap prototypes beat a locked-in wrong architecture.

**Apply when:** Greenfield module, unknown API, or the plan lists "figure out approach."

**Do:** Spike read-only or throwaway. Compare on proof, scope, and failure modes. Pick one; delete the rest.

**Don't:** Merge all three into a compromise.

**Report:** Options tried and the criterion that picked the winner.
