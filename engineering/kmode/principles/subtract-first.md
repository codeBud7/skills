# subtract-first

Remove dead weight before building on it.

**Why:** New code on obsolete adapters, flags, or comments doubles the system. Readers pay both.

**Apply when:** Touched area has unused branches, duplicate helpers, or leftover compatibility.

**Do:** Delete or fold the obsolete piece, then design what remains.

**Don't:** Stack a new adapter beside three that should have died.

**Report:** What you deleted first, and what that made unnecessary.
